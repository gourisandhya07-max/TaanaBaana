# 🧵 Taana-Baana
### From Hands to Markets.

Taana-Baana is an AI-powered digital business companion designed to help
artisans, weavers, handicraft makers, and marginalized micro-entrepreneurs
take their traditional products into the digital marketplace.

## 🌱 The Problem

Artisans create valuable products, but reaching digital markets can be
challenging due to:

- Limited digital literacy
- Language barriers
- Difficulty taking professional product photographs
- Lack of skills to create online product listings
- Uncertainty about competitive pricing
- Limited access to B2B and digital buyers
- Dependence on temporary exhibitions and physical fairs

Taana-Baana bridges this gap by simplifying the entire digital selling journey.

## 💡 Our Solution

Taana-Baana acts as a virtual AI business manager for artisans.

### The journey:

📸 **Capture** → Take a product photo  
✨ **Enhance** → AI creates a professional product image  
🎙️ **Speak** → Describe the product in your regional language  
📝 **Catalog** → AI generates professional product details  
💰 **Price** → AI provides explainable pricing suggestions  
🤝 **Connect** → Reach customers and B2B buyers  
📦 **Manage** → Track products, enquiries, inventory and sales

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. AI Product Studio
- AI-powered background removal
- Lighting and image enhancement
- Smart cropping and formatting
- E-commerce-ready product images

### 2. Multilingual AI Cataloger
- Voice-first product entry
- Regional-language speech input
- English and Hindi translation
- AI-generated titles, descriptions and keywords
- Minimal typing for low-literacy users

### 3. Smart Pricing Assistant
- Considers raw material and labour costs
- Considers packaging and other expenses
- Provides market-aware pricing suggestions
- Shows minimum, recommended and premium price ranges
- Explains the factors behind the recommendation

### 4. Digital Market Linkage
- Digital product catalogs
- B2B buyer discovery
- Buyer enquiries
- Quotation generation
- Shareable product listings

### 5. AI Business Dashboard
- Inventory tracking
- Sales monitoring
- Enquiry management
- Profit insights
- Demand and product performance insights

## 🎯 Who Is It For?

Taana-Baana is designed primarily for:

- Artisans
- Weavers
- Handicraft makers
- Self-help groups
- Micro-entrepreneurs
- Artisan clusters and cooperatives

Especially users who have limited digital skills or limited access to
professional digital commerce tools.

## 🌟 What Makes Taana-Baana Different?

Taana-Baana is not simply another online marketplace.

It combines:

**AI Photography + Voice Commerce + Multilingual Cataloging +
Smart Pricing + Market Linkage**

into one simple workflow.

The goal is to let artisans focus on what they do best — **creating** —
while AI handles much of the complexity of digital commerce.

## 🧵 Why "Taana-Baana"?

"Taana-Baana" refers to the interwoven threads that form a fabric.

The name represents the idea of weaving together:

**Traditional Craft + AI + Markets + Opportunity**

Just as individual threads come together to create something stronger,
Taana-Baana connects artisans with the digital ecosystem.
