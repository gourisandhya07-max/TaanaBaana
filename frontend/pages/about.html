The story behind Taana-Baana

Weaving tradition into opportunity.

Why Taana-Baana?

The craft already exists. The digital bridge should too.

Across India, artisans and craftspeople create products carrying generations of knowledge, culture and identity. Yet access to markets can remain limited by digital literacy, language barriers, product photography, catalog creation and pricing challenges.

Physical exhibitions and trade fairs can create valuable opportunities, but those opportunities are periodic. Taana-Baana is designed around a different idea: helping artisans build a digital presence that remains available beyond an exhibition date.

Instead of expecting every artisan to become a photographer, copywriter, translator, pricing analyst and digital marketer, Taana-Baana brings those capabilities into one simple workflow.

The challenge

A great handmade product does not automatically become a great digital product listing.

  • Limited year-round market access
  • Difficulty creating professional product photographs
  • Language and digital-literacy barriers
  • Uncertainty around competitive pricing
  • Time-consuming catalog creation
✨

The Taana-Baana approach

Give the artisan a simple digital business companion while keeping the artisan in control.

  • Photograph once and create a polished product presentation
  • Speak naturally in a regional language
  • Generate multilingual catalog content
  • Understand material, labour and market-based pricing factors
  • Share products with buyers digitally
